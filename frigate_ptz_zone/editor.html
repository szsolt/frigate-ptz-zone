Tap the picture to centre the camera there. Drag the points. Tap a small dot to add a point, double-tap a point to remove it. Points on the frame edge mean "continues off-screen".
Pan → across, tilt ↓ down. Green: keyframes. Yellow: camera. Red: no zone there, capture a keyframe nearby. Orange: beyond the last keyframe, a keyframe here pins the zone down. Orange or red dot: its neighbours disagree with that keyframe, redraw it or add one between. Tap to move the camera there. Hold an arrow to move; the camera stops when you let go. During and just after a move the zone shrinks for a few seconds: Frigate detects on frames a little behind the camera.
